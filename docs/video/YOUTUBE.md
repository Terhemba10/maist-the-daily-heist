## Description

Maist: The Daily Heist is a turn-based stealth puzzle you play right inside a Reddit post. Every day
there is a new heist, the same for everyone: sneak a crew of three past security bots, crack the vault
on three dream layers, race the ghost of tonight's #1 player, and if you get stuck, friends rescue you
by replying WAKE UP in the thread.

Play it: https://www.reddit.com/r/MaistHeist/

Built for Reddit's Game of the Day Hackathon 2026, on Reddit's Developer Platform (Devvit).

Music: "Pop Pop" by The_Mountain (Pixabay). Narration: Microsoft neural voice.
